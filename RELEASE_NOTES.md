notepad mint 1.8.0

- alt+click adds another cursor and alt+drag adds another selection with its own cursor. typing, backspace, delete, enter, cut, copy and paste work at all of them, and the arrow keys, home and end move them together. alt+click on a cursor removes it, esc goes back to one
- alt+up and alt+down move the current line, or the selected lines, up and down (also in the edit menu)
- copying from several selections puts each on its own line, and pasting that many lines gives one to each cursor
- one undo takes an edit back at every cursor and returns the cursors to where they were
- resizing with show invisibles on is fast again. in a long word-wrapped file a resize step took about 400 ms and now takes about 5, and a keystroke went from 90 ms to 10

**download**

- `notepad-mint-1.8.0-setup.exe`: installer and uninstaller
- `notepad-mint-1.8.0.exe`: standalone executable
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: download checksums

settings remain in `%appdata%\notepad-mint\settings.ini`.
